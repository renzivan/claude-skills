The Zewify mark

A Z drawn as a folded strip of paper: two solid bars joined by a lighter fold. It suggests calm, careful work and folds neatly into a square.

The mark on a green tile. It works as a favicon, an app-style icon and a share image without any changes.

Rules

The wordmark is set in Newsreader Medium with −0.02em tracking, always in sentence case: "Zewify", never "ZEWIFY". Keep clear space of half the tile width around the mark. The fold is 55% of the mark colour and is never a second hue.
